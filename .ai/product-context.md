# Product Context — ITPro

## Brand

ITPro — Inteligencia Tecnológica Profesional.

## Target customer

Small and medium businesses that need to improve their operation with technology but do not necessarily have an internal software, automation, or infrastructure team.

## What ITPro sells

ITPro sells technology enablement and operational support, especially:

- Business process organization
- Internal tools
- Software solutions
- Automation
- Managed platforms
- Operational continuity
- Technology accompaniment
- Infrastructure support when needed

## What ITPro should not sound like

Avoid positioning ITPro as:

- Computer repair
- Generic IT support only
- Informal technician service
- Low-level break/fix service
- Hardware-first business

Infrastructure can be mentioned, but only as support for software, automation, and operational continuity.

## Tone

The tone must be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Direct
- Business-oriented
- Not overly corporate
- Not too technical for non-technical business owners

## Core narrative

Many businesses operate with scattered tools, manual processes, spreadsheets, repeated tasks, and fragile technology.

ITPro helps them organize, automate, implement better systems, and keep operations running through continuous support.

## Main CTA

The main conversion action is WhatsApp.

Use phrases like:

- Hablemos por WhatsApp
- Quiero mejorar mi operación
- Agenda una conversación
- Cuéntanos qué proceso quieres mejorar

## Case study policy

There is a real founding client in the textile sector.

For V1, use only generic wording unless there is formal authorization.

Do not publish:

- Client name
- Client logo
- Private screenshots
- Order data
- End-client data
- Credentials
- Unauthorized amounts
- Sensitive configuration
