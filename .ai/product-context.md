# Product Context — ITPro Web Services

## Positioning

ITPro is a professional technology partner for SMBs.

The brand should communicate technical capability, operational understanding, trust, and continuous support.

## Core message

Software y automatización para empresas que quieren operar mejor.

## Value proposition

ITPro combines technology, implementation, operational adaptation, and continuous support so companies can work with more order, control, and efficiency.

## Problems ITPro solves

- Manual processes.
- Disorganized follow-up through WhatsApp, Excel, or loose documents.
- Lack of visibility over orders, clients, tasks, or internal work.
- Lack of internal documentation.
- Disconnected tools.
- Neglected technology infrastructure.
- Need for continuous technology support without hiring a full internal IT team.

## Services

### Software and automation

Automation of repetitive tasks, forms, reports, dashboards, internal tools, scripts, and process integrations.

### Managed operational platforms

Implementation, adaptation, administration, documentation, backups, user management, and continuous improvement of operational platforms.

### Continuous technology operations

Monthly support, remote assistance, periodic reviews, documentation, recommendations, and continuous improvement.

### Specialized infrastructure support

Networks, internet connectivity, servers, backups, workstations, printers, internal services, and specialized maintenance.

## What ITPro should not look like

The website must avoid making ITPro look like:

- A generic computer repair shop.
- A cheap web design agency.
- A reactive support-only service.
- An unlimited custom software provider.
- A software reseller without support.
- A tool installer without follow-up.

## Commercial approach

ITPro will not publish rigid public pricing in V1.

The commercial flow is:

1. The client contacts ITPro through WhatsApp.
2. ITPro understands the client's operation.
3. ITPro performs an initial diagnosis.
4. ITPro proposes an adjusted solution.
5. The service is classified as a one-time project, monthly service, or mixed model.

Until explicit authorization exists, the first client case must be presented generically as a textile-sector company.

Do not use client name, logo, screenshots, internal data, financial details, credentials, or sensitive technical details.
